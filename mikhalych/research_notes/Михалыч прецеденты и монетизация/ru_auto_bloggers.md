# RU/CIS short-form auto explainer creators: precedents, growth and monetization (for «Гараж Михалыча»)

Method note (read first). Most YouTube numbers below are **primary data I collected on 14 Oct 2026** from public YouTube pages (channel header = subscribers / video count; `/shorts` tab = views of the **last 48 Shorts**, summarised as median/max). A second dataset is **266 top-viewed RU auto Shorts** returned by ~49 YouTube searches the project ran on 5–7 Oct 2026 (files in the session scratchpad, `ytres/*.txt`), whose channel owners I resolved via YouTube oEmbed on 14 Oct 2026. Those are **lifetime** views of the best search hits, not typical views. Telegram subscriber counts come from public `t.me/<channel>` pages (14 Oct 2026). Instagram, TikTok and VK Clips counters were **not** collected: Instagram needs a login, TikTok is restricted for RU, and TGStat / Telega.in were blocked (Cloudflare / 404). Caveat: YouTube has been throttled in Russia since mid-2024 and, per Inc. Russia, Roskomnadzor dropped the domain from its DNS in Feb 2026. So YouTube figures partly reflect CIS and VPN audiences, and the same creators now cross-post to VK Video, Rutube and Dzen ([Inc. Russia, 2026](https://incrussia.ru/share/druzya-na-medlennoj-ploshchadke-zachem-biznesu-rabotat-s-avtoblogerami/)).

## Who are the leading RU short-form auto explainer / myth / lifehack / mechanic creators in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
The RU auto niche is led by face-driven personalities, and most of them are attached to a real business: Ильдар (used-car selection, автоподбор), Асафьев (selection plus a driving school), Лиса Рулит (own car showroom plus insurance), Дубровский (dealership network), Гараж 54 (detailing centre plus prize promotions). In short-form specifically, the strongest explainer formats are «механик объясняет» (Denis МЕХАНИК, Гараж 54), car lawyers (Шумский, Ярдрей), and service stations or brands answering myth questions (АВТОМАТИКА, MBSEMENOV, Ходос Авто, Нефтьмагистраль, KERRY). I found **no verified large RU auto channel built on an animated or AI mascot**. Persona and faceless precedents exist only as brand or community channels, plus a few tiny «Михалыч»/«Петрович» namesakes.

### Inferences
- The strongest RU pattern is **expert or business first, media second**. The big winners monetise through a transaction business (car selection, car sales, legal services, a service station) rather than ad revenue alone. Михалыч's analogue would be a product (the mini-app), a tyre/wheel and oil sales funnel, or OSAGO and other insurance.
- The «persona без лица» space looks **uncontested at scale** in RU auto. That is an opportunity (no incumbent) and also an unproven model (no precedent showing that a cartoon mechanic can carry trust for car-selection or legal-type services).
- Михалыч's ~20k views per Instagram reel is about the median of mid-tier YouTube auto-Shorts channels with 300–600k subs: Ильдар's Shorts channel 31k, «Автомобильные лайфхаки» 28k, АвтоХак 25k. This cross-platform comparison is approximate.
- The recent collapse of Нефтьмагистраль (old hits of 1–2.7M versus a current median of 3.3k) suggests that the same «Опасно ли…?» question format decays once the obvious myths are used up or the algorithm stops pushing the channel. Fresh topics and a recurring character are needed to keep it going.

### Gaps
- Instagram Reels, TikTok and VK Clips subscriber and view counts for these creators were not collected (login walls, RU TikTok restrictions). Comparable Instagram-native RU auto explainer accounts were not identified.
- Telegram stats via TGStat or Telemetr were blocked. Only raw `t.me` subscriber counts are available, without reach or ER.
- For several channels I did not verify face vs. faceless status (Нефтьмагистраль, «Автомобильные лайфхаки»).
- No exact timeline of when Shorts-first channels such as Шумский's started (channel join dates were not extracted).

## What revenue streams do they use and what are known integration prices / CPM in the RU auto niche?

### Takeaway
The revenue streams are:
