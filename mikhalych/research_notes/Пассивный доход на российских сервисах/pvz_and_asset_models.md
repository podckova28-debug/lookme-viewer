## 1. ПВЗ of Wildberries, Ozon, Яндекс Маркет, СДЭК, Boxberry, Lamoda, Авито/Почта: requirements, upfront investment, revenue model, monthly profit, payback, and changes 2024–2026

### Takeaway
By 2026 a marketplace ПВЗ is a low-margin operating business whose economics depend on turnover, not a passive asset. Payouts are a % of the value of goods handed out: WB now around 2–3.5%+ after a roughly 25% cut in 2025, Ozon about 4.5–6%, Yandex Market 5% capped at 750 ₽ per item. Upfront costs run about 350–500k ₽, and monthly fixed costs of about 150–220k ₽ (rent plus 2 staff) eat most of the margin. A point with hired staff and turnover below about 4–5 m ₽/month can lose money. The advertised "8–12 months" payback applies only to strong locations.

The point must be open **at least 12 hours a day, every day**. Being closed for more than 30 minutes brings a **6,000 ₽ fine**. New partners in priority zones may get **+3% to the tariff or compensation for the signboard** — [search summary citing SelSup / rb.ru](https://selsup.ru/blog/wildberries-vvodit-izmeneniya-dlya-vladeltsev-punktov-vydachi/) (2026)

### Inferences
- Across all three major marketplaces the revenue model is the same: **% of the value of goods handed out (2–6%), sometimes with per-item caps, plus small per-return fees and time-limited opening subsidies.** A realistic turnover of 3–5 m ₽/month therefore gives about **100–250k ₽ of gross payout**, and two hired staff plus big-city rent (150–220k) consume most or all of it.
- The worked examples point to a clear dividing line. Profits of 80k+ in guides usually assume the owner staffs the counter, or rent at 30k in a small town. With two hired staff, the 2026 examples show about **−36k (WB) to +42k (Ozon)** per month.
- The trend from 2024 to 2026 is **falling unit payouts** (WB −25% in 2025; subsidies cut; Ozon pushing exclusivity), **rising deposits** (WB 50k, Ozon 30k), and **rising rents** (+15%). Payback periods have lengthened from the "8–12 months" of 2022–2023 marketing to 15–24 months or never at average locations.

## 2. Market saturation and conflicts: number of ПВЗ, closures, protests over fines and tariffs, tariff cuts, multi-brand policies, own ПВЗ, постаматы

### Takeaway
Russia had about 226k ПВЗ on 1 Jan 2026 (+45% YoY) and about 260k by September 2026, with growth slowing to about +25% (+12% in million-plus cities). The market is near saturation. Three shocks hit in mid-2026: WB's 2025 payout cut, drone attacks on WB warehouses in July 2026 that halved flows to points, and payment delays. Together they triggered a wave of ПВЗ sale listings and demands for rent cuts. Ozon is now paying to lock in mono-brand points, while the platform-economy law (289-ФЗ, from 1 Oct 2026) bars one-sided changes of terms but does not directly ban mono-brand requirements.

DNS bought PickPoint postamats at a **20% discount** — [New Retail](https://new-retail.ru/business/azhiotazh_vokrug_postamatov_ozhidaniya_i_realnost7568/); [Logirus](https://logirus.ru/news/e-commerce/dns_kupila_postamaty_pickpoint_s_20-protsentnym_diskontom.html)

### Inferences
- The market has moved from land grab (2022–2025) to **consolidation**: slower openings, more exits, and pressure on per-point turnover as points cannibalize each other. In million-plus cities growth has almost stopped (+12%).
- The July 2026 WB crisis shows a risk specific to Russia in 2026: **physical and logistics disruption upstream of the ПВЗ.** It is outside the owner's control and cuts turnover-based income directly, while rent and wages stay fixed.
- Ozon's exclusivity bonus and WB's history of one-sided оферта changes show the core structural risk: **the counterparty can rewrite the tariff at any time.** 289-ФЗ may limit unilateral changes from October 2026, but its enforcement is untested.
- Postamats have not been a retail-investor asset class. Operators own them, and the sector shrank after PickPoint's exit.

### Gaps
- No verified information was found on a distinct "2024 WB owners' protest". The well-documented strike was March 2023; 2025 saw tariff complaints, and 2026 saw rent/sale actions rather than a strike. The report should not attribute a strike to 2024 without a source.
- Official 2026 counts of closed (not just listed-for-sale) ПВЗ were not found.
- No Data Insight 2026 ПВЗ report was accessible.
- There is no 2026 postamat count.

## 3. How "passive" is a ПВЗ: owner involvement vs hired staff, staff costs, theft/substitution fines, rent risk

### Takeaway
A ПВЗ is only "passive" if you accept a thin or negative margin. With 2 hired operators (about 80–117k ₽/month payroll) and city rent (50–100k), most 2026 examples show 0 to about 40k ₽ net, or a loss. The profitable cases are owner-operated or multi-point networks with an active manager. The owner also carries uncapped liability for substitutions, losses and fines, plus fixed rent against falling, volatile turnover.

### Cited Findings
- Staff cost benchmarks (2026): **2 employees = 80,000 ₽/month** (Ozon example) — [Postavleno](https://postavleno.ru/blog/vygodno-li-otkryt-punkt-vydachi-ozon/); **2 × 50,000 = 100,000 ₽** (Ozon big-city guide) — [1000bankov](https://1000bankov.ru/wiki/rukovodstvo-po-zapusku-pvz-ozon-ot-oformleniya-dokumentov-do-polucheniya-garantirovannogo-dohoda/); **ФОТ 117,000 ₽** (WB example) — [finance.mail.ru](https://finance.mail.ru/article/franshiza-wildberries-262/)
- Operating obligations: WB requires **≥12 hours/day, 7 days a week**, and fines **6,000 ₽ for closure >30 min** — [МТС Банк](https://www.mtsbank.ru/articles/kak-otkryt-pvz-wildberries/) (2026). A 7-day, 12-hour schedule effectively needs 2+ operators working shifts, or the owner.
- Liability: WB charges **full item value** for substitution, late hand-back or loss, plus fines for late acceptance, courier loss, "unfair competition" and use of third-party software — [МТС Банк](https://www.mtsbank.ru/articles/kak-otkryt-pvz-wildberries/); [Модульбанк](https://delo.modulbank.ru/pickup-point/Wildberries-fines-pvz-owners); [TotalCRM on the WB Point оферта](https://totalcrm.ru/blog/2026/01/oferta-wb-point-i-promezhutochnye-servisy-prakticheskoe-rukovodstvo-dlya-partnerov-i-vladelcev-pvz-aktualno-na-2026-god_141) (Jan 2026)
- Fraud against ПВЗ: attackers **hijack accounts, file fake returns of expensive goods and disappear with both goods and money** — [Prochepetsk](https://prochepetsk.ru/news/74270) (2026, regional outlet)
- Rent risk: rents rose **about 15% by end-2025** — [Retail.ru](https://www.retail.ru/news/v-2025-godu-wildberries-sokratil-vyplaty-vladeltsam-pvz-na-25-24-aprelya-2026-277136/). If WB withdraws approval of the location or ends the contract, **the business stops but the lease obligation remains** — [logistics.ru / Freedome search summary](https://freedome-realty.ru/blog/trebovaniya-setevikov/kak-sdat-pomeshchenie-v-arendu-dlya-punkta-vydachi-wildberries-trebovaniya-protsess-i-sovety/) (2026)
- ПВЗ "increasingly resembles a low-margin operational asset where an error in location or rent can kill the project's economics" — [logistics.ru](https://logistics.ru/internet-torgovlya-i-fulfilment-riteyl-upravlenie-operaciyami/pvz-wildberries-teryayut-dokhodnost) (2026)
- A seller of a Moscow "network of profitable WB points" claims **550k ₽/month net profit** — [Altera Invest listing](https://alterainvest.ru/msk/products/detail/16426486/) (a sale listing; the seller's claim is not verified)

### Inferences
- Rough 2026 arithmetic from the cited examples: payout of about 3.5–5% on 4 m ₽ turnover gives 140–200k. Subtract about 80–117k staff, 50–100k rent and about 10–25k taxes/other, and the **hired-staff case lands around −50k to +50k/month**. That is roughly a **0–15% annual return on 400k ₽ of capital**, with high variance and principal at risk. The owner-operator case earns more, but it is a job, not passive income.
- Buying an existing point in 2026 carries adverse-selection risk. Listings surged after the July crisis, and sellers report historical figures that may no longer hold.

### Gaps
- No systematic data on the average monthly fines paid per point.
- No data on employee theft rates.
- No survey of what share of owners run points hands-off with staff.

## 4. Other asset-based ecosystem models: taxi-car rental, darkstore premises, vending/postamats, retailer franchises (Пятёрочка), "Яндекс Маркет/Ozon franchise", and crowdlending to marketplace sellers

### Takeaway
None of the alternatives is truly passive either:
- **Taxi-car rental** yields about 45–90k ₽/month gross per car before depreciation and repairs, and the 2026 localization law shrinks the pool of eligible cars.
- **Darkstore premises (ГАБ with Самокат)** are the most passive option, but they need tens of millions of rubles and pay back in about 9–12 years.

## 5. Scams: "инвестируй в ПВЗ / товар на WB — получай 5% в месяц" and how to recognize them

### Takeaway
The Bank of Russia flags any promised return far above deposit rates as a key pyramid marker. Typical pyramids promise 20–30% a month, but more "plausible" offers of 3–10% a month (36–120%+ a year) are also far above any legitimate ПВЗ or seller economics shown above. The scale is large: 20k+ pyramid sites blocked in 2025, and 656 pseudo-investment projects detected in Q1 2026 alone. I found no specific, well-documented court case on a "ПВЗ-investment pyramid" in the accessible sources.

### Cited Findings
- CB/banks: pyramid organizers typically promise **20–30% a month (240–360% a year)**. Some "investment platforms" promise **30–40% a month**, pay the first returns to build trust, then disappear — [ББР Банк](https://bbr.ru/landings/infobezpiramida); [Экспобанк](https://expobank.ru/blog/kak-raspoznat-moshennikov-finansovye-piramidy/)
- **In 2025 more than 20,000 pyramid-related websites were blocked** — [Выберу.ру](https://www.vbr.ru/help/novosti/lovushki-finansovyh-piramid-88871/). **In Q1 2026 the CB identified 1,400 companies with signs of illegal activity, including 656 pseudo-investment projects** — same search summary. **Jan–Apr 2025: 1,638 companies with pyramid signs** — [Ведомости, 6 May 2025](https://www.vedomosti.ru/finance/news/2025/05/06/1108557-viyavil-finansovih-piramid)
- CB red flags: no license or registry entry (check the [CB Справочник финансовых организаций](https://www.cbr.ru/faq/pnp/)), and returns far above bank offers — [Банк России FAQ](https://www.cbr.ru/faq/pnp/)
- Telegram-bot investment scams: fake dashboards showing "profit", then pressure to invest more or take a loan. The МВД warned about them in **Oct 2025** — [РБК, 12 Oct 2025](https://www.rbc.ru/society/12/10/2025/68eb56d69a794720874bcd7e). "Passive income" offers promise **50–100k ₽/month** with "no risk" — [BeInCrypto](https://ru.beincrypto.com/moshenniki-vps-v-telegram/)
- Marketplace-themed scams: fake Ozon look-alike sites used in "boost product rating" earning schemes, with one victim losing **about 200k ₽** — [iGuides / progorod43 summary](https://www.iguides.ru/main/os/5_kovarnykh_lovushek_moshennikov_na_wildberries_i_ozon_o_kotorykh_dolzhen_znat_kazhdyy/). "Invest in marketplace goods" offers circulate as private-investment listings (e.g. [beboss project listing](https://www.beboss.ru/investments/projects/6264-tovarnyy-biznes-na-marketpleysah)). Ozon Банк publishes guidance on investment scams — [Ozon Банк](https://finance.ozon.ru/blog/b2c/article/kak-raspoznat-moshennichestvo-v-investiciyah?attempt=1)
- Unverified "franchise + guaranteed yield" sites exist, e.g. **x5-invest.ru ("доходность от 20% в год")**, not on X5's official domain — [x5-invest.ru](http://x5-invest.ru/)

### Inferences
Recognition checklist, derived from the sources above:
1. **Promised fixed monthly return.** Section 1 shows that a real ПВЗ earns a volatile, often negative margin. "5%/month" (60%+ a year) on ПВЗ or WB goods is not supported by marketplace economics, and neither is 20–30%/month.
2. **Nobody legitimate sells "a share in a ПВЗ"**: WB, Ozon and Yandex Market contract only with the operating ИП/ООО. There is no franchise fee; only a 30–50k deposit is paid to the marketplace itself.
3. **Offers to "invest in goods on WB"** without equity, a loan agreement on a CB-registered investment platform, or sight of the seller's cabinet are unregulated private loans at best.
4. **Channel and payment red flags:** Telegram bots, payments to personal cards or crypto, pressure to reinvest or take a loan, early "payouts".
5. **Legitimate alternative:** CB-registered platforms (JetLend, Поток and others). Even there, sector overdue was above 22% at end-2025.

### Gaps
- I found no named, documented case (court or CB warning list) of a pyramid specifically marketed as "investment in ПВЗ" or "WB product investment at X%/month". Such schemes are widely discussed anecdotally, but the accessible sources gave no citable specifics.
- The CB's warning list could not be searched directly (cbr.ru blocked).
