## 1. Which services run their own (in-house) programs, and what they are called

### Takeaway
By September 2026 most big Russian ecosystems run their **own** programs for individuals. These include Яндекс Маркет (Реферальная программа / Партнёрская сеть Маркета), Ozon (Ozon Blogger, Ozon Тренды), Wildberries (only through its video platform Wibes), ВкусВилл, Купер (cpa.kuper.ru), Яндекс Go/Еда, Яндекс Плюс/Кинопоиск, Литрес, Сбер (Партнёрская программа Сбера), Т-Банк (Т-Партнёры) and the banks' «Приведи друга» schemes. Lamoda, X5 (Впрок, Пятёрочка Доставка), М.Видео-Эльдорадо, Золотое Яблоко, Детский мир and Спортмастер appear to work **only through CPA networks** (Admitad, Advcake, Gdeslon, EPN, Pampadu).

## 2. Commission structure: rates, fixed payouts, attribution windows, "new customer" rules

### Takeaway
Rates vary widely by type of service:
- **Marketplaces:** a percentage of the item price that depends on category. Яндекс Маркет pays up to 20% (older network figures say 1–14%). Ozon Blogger pays "up to 50%" in some categories. Wibes pays about 5–9.9%.
- **E-grocery:** usually a **fixed fee per first order** of roughly 100–700 ₽. ВкусВилл is the exception with 8% for 90 days.
- **Books:** 20–25%.
- **Fashion and beauty in CPA networks:** 3–10%.
- **Banks:** a **fixed 500–30 000 ₽ per product action**, which in absolute rubles per conversion is by far the highest.

### Cited Findings

**Яндекс Маркет**
- **Buyer and blogger referral (from 21 Aug 2025):** up to **20% of the price of each item** bought through the link, paid **once a month** in rubles — [Яндекс 21.08.2025](https://yandex.ru/company/news/21-08-2025-02); [AdIndex 21.08.2025](https://adindex.ru/news/digital/2025/08/21/336385.phtml); [CNews](https://www.cnews.ru/news/line/2025-08-21_pokupateli_yandeks_marketa); [Retail.ru](https://www.retail.ru/rbc/pressreleases/pokupateli-yandeks-marketa-smogut-zarabatyvat-rubli-za-rekomendatsii-tovarov/)
- **Partner network rates (aggregator descriptions):** CPA **1–14% of order value**, depending on product category and on whether the client is new or existing. Some aggregators add a CPC component of 45–60% of the click value and "up to 2000 ₽ per order". These are third-party figures and may be outdated (устар.?) — [aff1.ru](https://aff1.ru/affiliates/yandex-market/); [Pampadu blog](https://pampadu.ru/blog/5991-partnerskaya-programma-yandeks-marketa/); [PRO CPA гайд 2026](https://procpa.media/articles/partnerskaya-programma-yandeks-market-gajd-dlya-arbitrazhnikov-2026/)

One secondary source gives "14 days for new / 7 days for already registered" users, but that seems to mix in the РСЯ referral program — **unconfirmed** — [PRO CPA](https://procpa.media/articles/partnerskaya-programma-yandeks-market-gajd-dlya-arbitrazhnikov-2026/)

  - **15 000 ₽ per seller**, up to 150 000 ₽ for 10 sellers.
  - Eligible invitees are ИП or organizations that have never sold on Маркет, or have had no goods on the storefront for 90+ days.

## 3. Who can join, payout thresholds and payout methods

### Takeaway
Programs that pay **rubles** now almost always require at least **самозанятый (НПД) status**; many accept ИП and ООО too. Programs paying in **points** (Ozon Тренды, the original Яндекс Маркет referral, some ВкусВилл bonuses, bank «Приведи друга» cash to own account) are open to ordinary individuals. Bank «Приведи друга» cash goes to the client's own account with no status needed, but bank *agent* programs want самозанятый, ИП or юрлицо status.

### Cited Findings
- **Яндекс Маркет (rubles):**
  - Participants must register as **самозанятый, ИП or юрлицо** and accept an offer agreement in the cabinet.
  - Payouts are **monthly**.
  - Without that status, rewards come as Плюс points.

  - Самозанятые pay НПД at 6% by the 25th of the following month.
  - [yandex.ru/support/market-distr/ru/self-employed](https://yandex.ru/support/market-distr/ru/self-employed); [aff1.ru](https://aff1.ru/affiliates/yandex-market/)

## 4. Restrictions: traffic sources, brand bidding, cashback/motivated traffic, ad labeling

### Takeaway
Almost all programs **ban brand bidding in contextual ads** (Яндекс Маркет, Lamoda, Золотое Яблоко), spam, bots and misleading "fake discount" creatives. Cashback and coupon traffic is either banned or paid at a much lower rate. Since 2023–2025, Russian law requires ad labeling with an **erid** token and, from **1 Apr 2025**, a **3% levy** on online ad revenue that reaches individual blogger-distributors, including самозанятые. Ozon Blogger and Яндекс Маркет now handle labeling in-app.

### Cited Findings
- **Яндекс Маркет:**
  - **Brand context** in ads (e.g. «Яндекс Маркет», «купить на маркете») leads to rejected orders and a **blocked platform**.
  - Comment spam, e-mail without consent and bot inflation mean automatic termination.
  - Anti-fraud flags traffic spikes, identical IPs and sessions under 10 seconds.
  - [Требования к источникам трафика](https://yandex.ru/support/market-distr/ru/rules.html); [PRO CPA](https://procpa.media/articles/partnerskaya-programma-yandeks-market-gajd-dlya-arbitrazhnikov-2026/)

  - Fines reach hundreds of thousands of rubles, and up to 1 million ₽ for repeat offenses by legal entities.
  - [skorozvon](https://skorozvon.ru/articles/vsyo-chto-nuzhno-znat-o-pravilah-markirovki-reklamy-v-internete); [ОРД-А](https://ord-a.ru/help/wiki/korotko-o-novom-zakone/); [salid.ru](https://salid.ru/journal/zakon-o-markirovke-reklamy-kak-partnyoram-salid-markirovat-reklamu)
- **Legal: 3% levy.** From **1 Apr 2025**, advertising distributors pay **3% of quarterly internet-ad revenue** aimed at Russian users. This covers companies, ИП **and самозанятые** — [skorozvon](https://skorozvon.ru/articles/vsyo-chto-nuzhno-znat-o-pravilah-markirovki-reklamy-v-internete); [mgutu.ru](https://mgutu.ru/media/advertising/markirovka-reklamy/)

### Inferences
- **Brand bidding, cashback/motivated traffic and SEO for "промокод + brand" are the most common bans.** They protect the advertiser's own channels. Open social or blog content with a personal link is the "safe" traffic type.
- **In-house blogger programs take on part of the compliance burden.** Ozon Blogger and Яндекс Маркет handle labeling in-app, which lowers the barrier compared with CPA-network offers where the partner must get an erid.
- **Hold/rejection risk is real.** User complaints about Яндекс Маркет suggest that part of the reported earnings never gets paid.

### Gaps
- **Unverified whether the 3% levy applies** to rewards under in-house programs where the platform itself is the advertiser/distributor. It is unclear whether Ozon or Яндекс pay it on the blogger's behalf.
- **Full forbidden-source lists** for Ozon Blogger, Wibes, ВкусВилл, Купер and Т-Партнёры were not retrieved.

## 5. How much partners actually earn, and which verticals pay best

### Takeaway
Hard, independent earnings data is scarce. Most figures come from company PR or small case studies. The published examples are:
- **ВкусВилл:** 75k+ partners.
- **Яндекс Маркет:** 80 million points paid out in the first ~2 months, with a record of 5 million points for one participant.
- **Anecdotes:** 20 000–445 000 ₽ earned by individuals.

**Banks and fintech clearly pay the most per conversion.** They pay a fixed 1 000–7 000 ₽ per friend, up to 30 000 ₽ per business client and ~2–3k ₽ per business account. E-grocery pays ~100–700 ₽ per first order. Marketplaces pay a percentage of item price that often works out to tens of rubles per order.

### Inferences
- **Ranking by reward per conversion:** banks/fintech (1–30k ₽) > HR/courier recruiting (3–49k ₽, but it needs the recruit to work 100–200 hours) > e-grocery first orders (100–700 ₽) > books (20–25%) > fashion/beauty (3–10%) > marketplaces (percentage of often cheap items; realistic blended rate likely a few %).
- **Marketplaces win on conversion volume and audience fit.** Banks win on the check but have low conversion and strict "new client" rules.
- **Most published earnings are company PR or survivorship-biased cases.** A typical small blogger or ordinary person should expect pocket money (thousands to low tens of thousands of rubles a month). "Passive income" at scale needs a large audience or an SEO/content business.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of the distribution of partner earnings (median income) was found for any program.
- No published data on Ozon Blogger or Wibes participant numbers or payout totals as of Sept 2026.
- Т-Партнёры payout volumes and partner counts were not found.

## 6. Recent changes, 2024–2026 (launches, closures, rate changes)

### Takeaway
2025–2026 saw marketplaces **launch in-house, ruble-paying blogger programs**:
- **Яндекс Маркет:** points in June 2025, rubles from August 2025.
- **Ozon Blogger:** Telegram from Dec 2025, VK and MAX from May 2026.
- **WB Wibes:** April 2025, with rates raised in June 2025.

Over the same period, regulation added the 3% ad levy (April 2025). Some CPA offers (Самокат, Детский мир, Яндекс Go installs) were suspended.

### Inferences
- **The direction is clear:** marketplaces are building their own creator-commerce programs (Ozon Blogger, Яндекс Маркет, Wibes). They tie these to domestic platforms (Telegram, VK, MAX, their own video feeds) and require формализация (НПД, ИП or ООО) for ruble payouts. This squeezes out classic CPA-network affiliates for marketplaces.
- **Ozon's MAX/VK expansion is policy-aligned.** It matches the state push toward the MAX messenger.

### Gaps
- **No reports of rate cuts found.** Confirmed rate cuts in 2024–2026 for Ozon, Яндекс Маркет, Lamoda or Золотое Яблоко were not found; this is not proof there were none.
- **Status of the older Яндекс Маркет partner network unclear.** It is not confirmed whether the separate CPC/CPA network (aff.market.yandex.ru) still runs under the old model or has been fully merged into the "Реферальная программа".
