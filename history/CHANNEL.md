# Оформление канала (история, англ.)

## Аватарка
Варианты в `avatar/` (800×800 PNG, YouTube сам обрежет в круг). Без текста, простой символ, читается в маленьком кружке.

## Описание канала (вставить в «Настройки канала → Основные сведения → Описание»)
Whole histories, told in minutes.

Rasputin, secret societies, spies, empires and the people who broke them. Every video tells one complete story from the first day to the last, with hand-drawn style animation, real dates and real sources, and no filler.

New episodes every week. Subscribe so you don't miss the next one.

Sources for every episode are listed in the video description.

## Ключевые слова канала (Настройки → Канал → Ключевые слова)
history, history documentary, animated history, history explained, entire history, history in minutes, short history, world history, european history, russian history, secret societies, famous people in history, historical figures, biography, true stories, dark history, mysteries of history, espionage history, empires, history channel
