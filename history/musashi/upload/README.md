## Описание
April 1612. On a tiny island, Japan's most famous swordsman waits with a blade so long they call it the Drying Pole. His opponent arrives two hours late, carrying a sword he carved from a boat oar during the crossing. A few seconds later, the champion is lying on the sand.

This is the entire life of Miyamoto Musashi: the wild boy who won his first duel at thirteen, fought more than sixty times without a single defeat, and then wrote a book in a cave that business people still buy today.
