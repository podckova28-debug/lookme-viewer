He is born around eleven sixty two, beside the Oh-non River, in the grasslands of what is now Mongolia.
His name is Teh-moo-jin.
According to The Secret History of the Mongols, he comes into the world clutching a clot of blood in his fist, and the old women say it means he will be a great warrior.
His father, Yeh-soo-gay, is a minor chief.
His mother, Hoh-eh-loon, was kidnapped by Yeh-soo-gay from a Mer-kit warrior, on the way home from her own wedding.
The Merkits will not forget it.
When Teh-moo-jin is nine, his father takes him to another clan to choose a bride.
He is engaged to a girl named Bor-teh, and left behind to live with her family.
On the way home, Yeh-soo-gay stops at a camp of Tah-tars, old enemies, who offer him food.
A few days later, he is dead.
They have poisoned him.
And the boy is about to learn that his own people are no safer.
