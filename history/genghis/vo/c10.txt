He has heard of a famous Chinese Taoist monk, Chahng-choon, who is said to be over three hundred years old.
Jeng-giss Khan sends for him, and the old monk travels for more than a year, across deserts and mountains, to reach the khan's camp in the Hin-doo Koosh.
In twelve twenty two, Jeng-giss Khan asks him the question he has been waiting to ask.
Do you have the medicine of immortality?
The monk answers honestly.
There are ways to protect life, he says, but there is no medicine that makes a man live forever.
Jeng-giss Khan thanks him, gives him gifts and frees his monasteries from taxes.
Then he goes back to war.
He has one more enemy, a kingdom that broke its promise to send him troops.
And it will be his last campaign.
