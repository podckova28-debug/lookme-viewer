In twelve oh four, Teh-moo-jin defeats the Nai-mahns, the last great tribe of the west.
Jah-moo-kha, who fought with them, flees into the mountains with a handful of men.
In twelve oh five, those men tie him up and hand him over, hoping for a reward.
Teh-moo-jin has the traitors executed for betraying their lord.
Then, according to The Secret History, he offers his old blood brother friendship once more.
Jah-moo-kha refuses, and asks only to die without his blood being spilled, as a noble.
His wish is granted.
In the spring of twelve oh six, at a great assembly by the Oh-non River, under a white banner with nine horse-tail tassels, Teh-moo-jin is proclaimed ruler of all the people who live in felt tents.
His new title is Ching-giss Khan.
Jeng-giss Khan.
And now, he looks south.
