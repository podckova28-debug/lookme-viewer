Shee-non, March fourteen twenty nine.
Charles is twenty six, poor, hesitant, and not even sure that he is the true son of the old king.
According to the story, he hides among his courtiers to test her, while another man stands in his place.
Joan walks past the impostor, goes straight to Charles, and kneels.
In private, she tells him something that no one else hears.
Whatever it is, he believes her.
But he is careful.
For three weeks, at Pwah-tee-ay, churchmen question her about her voices.
Ladies of the court examine her to confirm that she is a virgin.
Their verdict: they find nothing evil in her, and she should be put to the test.
And the test they choose is the most dangerous place in France.
