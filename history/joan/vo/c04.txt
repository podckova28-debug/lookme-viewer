The city of Or-lay-on, on the Lwahr, has been under English siege since October fourteen twenty eight.
If it falls, the road to the south is open.
Joan is given a suit of armour, horses and a small household.
She has a banner made, white, with Christ holding the world, and the names Jesus and Maria.
Legend says that the sword she asks for is found buried behind the altar of a church at Fyair-bwah, marked with five crosses.
Then she sends a letter to the English commanders.
She cannot write, so she dictates it.
Go home to England, it says, or I will make you go.
On the twenty ninth of April, fourteen twenty nine, she rides into Or-lay-on at night, and the whole city comes out with torches to see her.
The English laugh at her letter.
This channel tells whole lives in a few minutes.
If you want the next one, subscribe.
Because in nine days, a girl who has never seen a battle is going to end a siege that has lasted seven months.
