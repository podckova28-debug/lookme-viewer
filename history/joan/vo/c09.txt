The trial opens in Roo-on, the English capital in France, in January fourteen thirty one.
The judge is Pierre Ko-shon, a French bishop who works for the English.
Facing her are dozens of theologians and lawyers.
She has no lawyer at all.
She is kept in a castle tower, in chains, guarded day and night by English soldiers.
Day after day, they try to trap her.
One question is designed so that any answer will condemn her.
Is she in the grace of God?
If she says yes, it is pride.
If she says no, she admits her guilt.
Joan answers: if I am not, may God put me there.
If I am, may God keep me there.
The judges cannot break her with questions.
So they try something else.
Fear.
