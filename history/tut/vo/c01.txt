He is born around thirteen forty one B C, in a brand new city in the desert called Ah-khet-ah-ten, today known as Ah-mar-nah.
His name is Too-tan-kah-ten, the living image of the Ah-ten.
His father, according to DNA studies, is almost certainly the pharaoh Ah-ken-ah-ten, the strangest king in Egyptian history.
Ah-ken-ah-ten has done something no pharaoh dared before.
He has abandoned the old gods of Egypt, Ah-moon, Osiris and hundreds of others, and ordered the country to worship just one: the Ah-ten, the disk of the sun.
He closed temples, sent away their priests, and moved the capital to this new city.
The boy grows up among sun-filled courtyards and images of his family under the rays of the sun.
His mother, the same DNA studies suggest, was his father's own sister.
But when Too-tan-kah-ten is still a small child, his father dies.
And within a few years, the boy is the only male heir left.
