Eye rules for only about four years.
After him, the army commander Hor-em-heb becomes pharaoh, and he sets out to erase the entire Ah-mar-nah family from history.
His workers break up the temples of the sun god, and use the stones as filling for new buildings.
The names of Ah-ken-ah-ten, of Eye, and of Too-tan-kah-moon are cut out of monuments.
Later lists of kings jump straight from the pharaoh before Ah-ken-ah-ten to Hor-em-heb, as if the boy king had never ruled at all.
About two hundred years later, workmen cutting a tomb for another pharaoh, Ram-eh-seez the Sixth, build their stone huts right on top of the entrance to Too-tan-kah-moon's tomb.
Their rubble covers the steps completely.
The king's enemies wanted him forgotten.
They succeed so well that the tomb robbers forget him too.
For more than three thousand years.
