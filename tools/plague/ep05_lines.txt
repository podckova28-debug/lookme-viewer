L02|NARRATOR|Your body uses vitamin C to make collagen, the glue that holds you together. It's in your skin, your gums, your blood vessels and your bones. And it's constantly being worn out and rebuilt.

L04|SCURVY|No germs. No fever. I simply take away the glue.
L05|NARRATOR|But where did he come from? Almost every animal makes its own vitamin C. Dogs, cats, goats, rats. They never get scurvy, whatever they eat.

L07|NARRATOR|They lived in forests full of fruit, so it didn't matter. The broken gene was passed down to monkeys, to apes, and to us. Guinea pigs and some bats lost it too.
