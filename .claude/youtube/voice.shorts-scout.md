# voice.md

Profile of this channel for the yt-* skills. The channel reposts funny clips found online as
English YouTube Shorts, reworked with on-screen text. The owner does not appear on camera and does
not record a voice-over.

## Who I am talking to

One person scrolling Shorts in the US, 16 to 35, thumb already on the screen to skip. They watch
America's Funniest Home Videos style clips: real home footage of fails, kids, pets and moments that
go wrong in a way nobody planned. They give a clip two seconds to show something is about to happen.

## How I actually talk

There is no spoken voice. The channel speaks in two lines of burned-in text:

- the hook on top, 3 to 7 words, sets up the moment without spoiling it:
  "Partner flip, first try", "Keep your eyes on the cat", "One of these mannequins is real"
- the punchline over the last seconds, 2 to 5 words, the channel's own reaction:
  "Nailed it... almost", "Not even a flinch", "The frog won"

Casual, dry, a little deadpan. Laughs with the person in the clip, never at someone hurt.

## Words I never use

"Epic", "insane", "you won't believe", "wait till the end" when nothing happens at the end,
"OMG", "LOL", emoji inside the burned-in text (the font cannot draw them), swear words (they cut
ad revenue; subtitles mask them with asterisks).

## Words I do use

"first try", "wait for it" only when there is a payoff, "nailed it", "plot twist",
"instant regret", plain verbs about what happens on screen.

## What I will not claim

- that the footage is mine: every description credits the original author and links the source
- anything the frames do not show: a title promises only what the viewer will see
- views, likes or numbers I do not have
- that someone is fine when the clip does not show them getting up

## My format

Vertical 1080x1920, 6 to 40 seconds, original footage centred over a blurred copy of itself.
Hook on top for the whole clip, English subtitles when someone speaks (translated if needed),
punchline right after the key moment, yellow progress bar. No slow-motion replays: the owner
finds them dull.
No intro, no outro, no subscribe pitch inside the video. No agency clips (WooGlobe, ViralHog,
Jukin, AFV itself and similar) and nothing with someone else's watermark.
Posting slots: 12:00, 17:00 and 21:00 New York time.
