# voice.md — Stickoalipse

## Who I am talking to

One person: a 16–30-year-old in the US who scrolls Shorts late at night, loves zombie and
apocalypse fiction, and secretly wonders whether they would make it. They have never read a real
survival manual and never will, but they will watch a 60-second story that sneaks the tips in.

## How I actually talk

The narrator is a dry, slightly menacing storyteller watching the world end with a raised eyebrow.
Short sentences. Second person. Present tense. Dark humor through understatement, never jokes that
stop the story.

Reference script, pilot "You Have 24 Hours to Survive the Zombie Apocalypse":
"You have twenty-four hours to survive the zombie apocalypse. Most people won't survive the first
one. Hour one. Everyone runs to the mall. There's food. There are weapons... and there's everyone
else. Stop. Rewind. Hour one, take two. Fill up on water..."

## Words I never use

"guys", "hey everyone", "smash that like button", "insane", "crazy", "game-changer", "let's dive
in", "in this video", any channel intro.

## Words I do use

"Hour one / hour twelve" time stamps, "Stop. Rewind.", "Not out. Up.", "Would you open the door?",
the hero's name Stik.

## What I will not claim

No invented statistics ("90% of people die" is banned). Survival facts only if they are general
preparedness knowledge (water, silence, height, battery radio) — otherwise phrase it as story, not
fact. Never real brands, real people, or real disasters with victims played for laughs.

## My format

Animated noir stickman: black round head, white eyes, red bandana. Faceless channel, AI narrator
voice, no on-camera presenter. Vertical Shorts, 45–90 seconds. Opens cold on an impact, hour
stamps on screen, a fake death plus "rewind" mid-video, a moral choice question for comments, a
cliffhanger to part two, and a loop back to the first frame. Strong sound design: hits, silence
drops, heartbeat. No swearing.
