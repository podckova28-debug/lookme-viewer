# Catching History · оформление канала

## Описание канала (YouTube → Настройка канала → Основные сведения → Описание)
```
Every disease has a story. We let the disease tell it.

Catching History is an animated series about the plagues, fevers and infections that shaped the world, from the Black Death to polio. In each episode the disease itself is the villain: it introduces itself, brags about its victories and explains what it actually does to the human body. Then we follow the people who fought back: the doctors, the scientists, the mistakes, the lucky breaks and the cures.

The ENTIRE History of… series: one disease per episode, the whole story in about ten minutes.
• Where it came from
• What it does to you
• How it changed history
• How we beat it (or didn't)

Hand-drawn stickman animation, real history, sources in every pinned comment.

New episodes regularly. Subscribe and find out who's next.
```
