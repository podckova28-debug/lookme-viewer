## Описание
```
The Jura mountains, France, November 1885. You're walking home with a loaf of bread when the neighbour's dog bites you. Twice. Once you feel the first symptom, it's already too late.

What the rabies virus does inside you, week by week, what your village would have done about it (a red-hot St Hubert's key and the hair of the dog that bit you), and the one choice you have: stay home, or take the train to Paris and let a chemist named Louis Pasteur inject you with dried rabbit spinal cord. Then: what happens if the same dog bites you today.
```
