## Description
By 2035, America is expected to have almost six million more jobs than today. But some jobs are going the other way — fast. A few could lose a third of their workers in just ten years.

The ten jobs shrinking fastest, ranked by the official U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projections for 2025–2035: sewing machine operators, camera repairers, file clerks, carpet installers, print binding workers, telemarketers, foundry mold makers, data entry keyers, switchboard operators and word processors & typists. And what they have in common: one task, repeated all day — exactly what machines and software do best.

These are projections, not certainties. Everyone in this video is a fictional illustration.
