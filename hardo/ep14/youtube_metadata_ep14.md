## Title
The Worst Jobs in History

Alternatives: "10 Worst Jobs in History (Counted Down)" · "History's Worst Jobs Make Yours Look Like a Holiday"

## Description
Before alarm clocks, somebody was paid to wake you up. Before toilets, somebody was paid to empty what was under you.

Ten of the worst jobs in history, counted down from bad to unbelievable: the knocker-upper, the leech collector, the mudlark, the pure finder, the whipping boy, the climbing boy, the gong farmer, the sin eater, the Groom of the Stool and the resurrection man. Plus one bonus job that was actually great.

Everyone in this video is a fictional illustration; the facts and numbers come from the sources below.

Sources:
• Knocker-uppers: long poles and pea shooters, a few pence a week per house; the last are thought to have retired in the 1970s — Wikipedia "Knocker-up"; Historic UK
• Leech collectors: up to ~2,500 leeches a day; France imported 30+ million leeches a year at the height of the craze — Science History Institute, Distillations; Wikipedia
• Mudlarks and pure finders — Henry Mayhew, London Labour and the London Poor (1851); Wikipedia
• Whipping boys: little firm evidence it really happened — Wikipedia; Britannica
• Climbing boys: 1788 minimum age of eight; ended by the Chimney Sweepers Act 1875 (police licensing) — UK Parliament, Living Heritage; Wikipedia
• Gong farmers: night work only; sixpence a day at a royal palace — Wikipedia; Historic UK
• Sin eaters: Welsh and border folklore; how common it was is debated — Wikipedia; Britannica
• Groom of the Stool — Historic Royal Palaces; Wikipedia
• Resurrection men: ~8 guineas a body in 1828; mortsafes; Anatomy Act 1832 — Wikipedia
• Cigar-factory lectores since the 1860s, paid by the workers — Araceli Tinajero, "El Lector" (2010); Wikipedia
