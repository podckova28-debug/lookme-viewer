## Title
Jobs That Will Appear in 10 Years (Some Already Exist)

Alternatives: "10 Jobs of the Future You've Never Heard Of" · "The Jobs of 2036, Explained"

## Description
In ten years, somebody will have a job that sounds like a joke today. Robot teacher. Heat officer. Deepfake detective. Some of these people are already at work.

Ten jobs of the next ten years, counted down from ten to one, plus a bonus twist: the robotaxi helper, the drone fleet supervisor, the deepfake detective, the battery recycler, the chief heat officer, the data center technician, the care robot technician, the wind turbine technician, the AI auditor and the robot trainer. These are forecasts, proposals and targets, not promises, but the first job ads are already out.

Every worker in this video is a fictional illustration; the jobs, rules and numbers come from the sources below.

Sources:
• 170 million jobs created and 92 million displaced by 2030; survey of 1,000+ employers; farmworkers the largest absolute growth (about +35 million) — World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report 2025
• Robotaxi remote assistance: helpers give the car advice, not control; about 70 on duty for about 3,000 vehicles — Waymo, "Advice, Not Control" (2026)
• Drones beyond visual line of sight: operations supervisor and flight coordinator roles, aircraft up to 1,320 lb — FAA proposed rule, Part 108 (Aug 2025)
• Deepfake labelling and chatbot disclosure from 2 August 2026 — EU AI Act, Article 50
• High-risk AI in hiring, credit and education, obligations from December 2027 — EU AI Act Annex III, as amended by the Digital Omnibus
• Lithium recovery 50% by end-2027 and 80% by end-2031; recycled content in new batteries from 2031 — EU Batteries Regulation 2023/1542
• First Chief Heat Officer: Miami-Dade County, 2021; later Athens, Freetown and Los Angeles — Miami-Dade County; Arsht-Rockefeller Foundation Resilience Center
• Data centre electricity to more than double by 2030 (about 945 TWh), slightly more than Japan uses today — IEA, Energy and AI (2025)
• Japan needs about 2.72 million care workers in FY2040; about 2.13–2.15 million today — Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare
• Wind turbine service technicians +60%, solar PV installers +48%, 2023–33, the two fastest-growing U.S. occupations — U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics
• Humanoid robot "data collection operator" ad: motion-capture suit, 7+ hours of walking a day, up to 30 lb, up to $48/hour, height 5'7"–5'11" — Business Insider (Aug 2024)
