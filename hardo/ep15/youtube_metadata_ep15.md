## Title
10 Jobs AI Can't Replace

Alternatives: "Jobs AI Still Can't Do (Even in 2026)" · "Who AI Can't Replace: 10 Jobs Based on Real Data"

## Description
AI can write essays, draw pictures and translate languages in seconds. So, is your job next?

We flipped the question: ten jobs AI can barely touch, counted down, based on real research. Roofers, plumbers, electricians, wind turbine technicians, phlebotomists, nurse practitioners, home health aides and more. And why the "safe" jobs are safe: hands, unpredictable places, and people who trust you.

Everyone in this video is a fictional illustration; the facts and numbers come from the sources below.

Sources:
• Microsoft Research, "Working with AI: Measuring the Occupational Implications of Generative AI" (Tomlinson et al., 2025, arXiv 2507.07935): ~200,000 Copilot conversations; least applicable jobs include phlebotomists, nursing assistants, hazardous materials removal workers, embalmers, roofers and massage therapists; most applicable include interpreters and translators, historians, writers and customer service
• U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment Projections 2024–34: wind turbine service technicians +49.9%; nurse practitioners +40.1%; electricians +9% (~77,400 jobs); home health and personal care aides +739,800 jobs (most of any occupation)
• World Economic Forum, Future of Jobs Report 2025: ~170 million jobs created and ~92 million displaced by 2030; fastest declining: postal clerks, bank tellers, data entry clerks
• Moravec's paradox — Hans Moravec, Mind Children (1988)
