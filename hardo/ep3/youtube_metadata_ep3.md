## Title
Why It's So Hard to Be a Teacher

## Description
It's 10:52 p.m. Notebook 117 of 150. The red pen is dying, and the essay is one sentence long.

This is one day as a teacher, lived from the inside: a copier with moods, twenty-eight kids and one asleep, fractions explained four ways, a twenty-minute lunch eaten next to the copier, a meeting about the next meeting, and a tote bag of notebooks that wears a seatbelt.

Based on real research:
• Full-time teachers work about 41 hours a week; only about 23 of them are teaching — OECD TALIS 2024
• Teachers spend close to $900 a year of their own money on the classroom — AdoptAClassroom Teacher Spending Survey, 2025
• 44% of new teachers leave within five years by one widely cited estimate; a federal cohort study found 17% — Ingersoll (UPenn); NCES 2015
