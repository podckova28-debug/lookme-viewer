## Описание
It's 1904, you're at the World's Fair in St. Louis, and a man hands you a beef patty between two slices of bread. No plate, no fork. Congratulations: you've just met the hamburger. Today it's the most famous sandwich on Earth — so who invented it? At least five people say they did, and none of them came from Hamburg.

The whole story: the Mongol saddle myth, Hamburg steak and the emigrant ships to New York, Hamburger Charlie, the Menches brothers, Old Dave, Oscar Bilby and Louis' Lunch (still no ketchup), The Jungle and the meat scandal of 1906, White Castle and the student who ate only burgers for 13 weeks, the McDonald brothers' Speedee kitchen and Ray Kroc, the cheeseburger, the Moscow queue of 1990, and a burger grown in a lab.
