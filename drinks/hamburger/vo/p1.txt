First, a legend.
You've probably heard that the hamburger started with Mongol warriors.
The story says they put raw meat under their saddles, and rode on it all day until it was tender.
Historians have looked for proof.
There isn't any.
And honestly, the horse would like a word.
The real story starts in the German port of Hamburg.
In the eighteen hundreds, Hamburg was famous for its beef.
Cooks there chopped it, mixed it with onions, and fried it.
Ships from Hamburg carried millions of emigrants to New York.
And they brought their food with them.
In America, the dish got a name.
Hamburg steak.
