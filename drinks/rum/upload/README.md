## Описание
You're a sailor in the British Navy, 1740. At noon a whistle blows, and an officer pours your daily ration: half a pint of rum. Every single day. But a hundred years earlier, this drink was literally trash.

How did the sticky black waste of the sugar trade become the drink of navies, pirates and presidents? This is the whole story: sugar and slavery, Old Grog and the limeys, the Rum Rebellion, the pirate song that pirates never sang, the molasses behind American independence, a deadly flood of syrup in Boston, Prohibition's Rum Row, Havana cocktails, tiki bars and Black Tot Day.
