Yo ho ho, and a bottle of rum... is not a real pirate song.
It comes from a novel, Treasure Island, written in eighteen eighty-three.
That's more than a hundred and fifty years after the golden age of piracy ended.
Real pirates drank whatever they could steal. Wine. Brandy. Beer.
Rum was simply the cheapest thing in the Caribbean.
But one real buccaneer did get famous for rum. Henry Morgan.
A Welsh privateer who raided Spanish cities, and then, instead of being hanged, was knighted and made lieutenant governor of Jamaica.
He spent his last years drinking. A lot.
Today his name is on one of the best-selling rums in the world.
Not bad for a pirate.
