## Описание
Paris, 1890, five o'clock: the green hour. Half the city is dripping ice water over sugar into glasses of bright green liquid. France drinks 36 million litres of it a year. Within 25 years it will be banned almost everywhere, accused of making people mad, blind and violent.

So what was really in that glass? This is the whole story: a Swiss herbal medicine, French soldiers in Algeria, the bug that killed France's wine, the green fairy of Van Gogh and Oscar Wilde, the doctor who poisoned guinea pigs, the 1905 murders that turned Europe against it, ninety-five years of secret "La Bleue", and the scientists who finally tested the old bottles.
