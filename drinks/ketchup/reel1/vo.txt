Ketchup was invented without tomatoes.
The original ketchup was fish.
Fermented fish sauce, from Southeast Asia.
British sailors loved it, and took it home.
Then English cooks made ketchup out of anything.
Mushrooms.
Walnuts.
Even beer.
Tomatoes? Most people thought they were poisonous.
When tomato ketchup finally won, America went so crazy, it sold tomato pills.
Which turned out to be laxatives.
So ketchup started as fish. And the tomato? It showed up last.
