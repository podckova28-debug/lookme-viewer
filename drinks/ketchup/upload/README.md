## Описание
It's 1690. You're a British sailor in Southeast Asia, and a merchant hands you a jar of brown, salty, slightly terrifying sauce. It smells like fish. Because it is fish. Congratulations: you've just met ketchup. No tomatoes. No sugar. No bottle.

So how did a fermented fish sauce become the red stuff on your fries? This is the whole ridiculous story: kê-tsiap from the South China Sea, English cooks making "ketchup" out of mushrooms, walnuts and oysters, the tomato that people thought might kill them, the 1830s tomato pills that were mostly laxatives, the gross factory ketchup and the Poison Squad, Henry Heinz and his not-actually-57 varieties, why nothing comes out of the bottle, and the year ketchup almost became a vegetable.
