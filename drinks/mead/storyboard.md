# Mead — storyboard v1 (for script v2)

Style for every frame (prefix in prompts): plain pure white background; detailed hand-drawn ink-line illustration with fine
cross-hatching and soft muted watercolour; characters/objects cut out cleanly on white, lots of white space; "you" = simple
white round-headed stick figure with dot eyes; hand-drawn black marker arrows + short handwritten notes (max 1–2 per frame).
Motion in edit: slow push-in / pan on each frame (6–12 s), marker notes fade in a beat after the line that sets them up.
